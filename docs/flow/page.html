From a vendor's website, or a Quote List with no estimate or revision yet, to priced lines in NetSuite

Where each step happens: on the vendor's own website with the customer, in the Consumers SmartAdd browser extension (Edge or Chrome), in the Product Selector on the sales PC, and in NetSuite. Part 2 starts a new estimate from the Quote List through NetSuite's Customer Search; part 3 starts a new Sales Order Revision with the Quote List on it. Click any numbered box to read its exact buttons.

Step by step

1Set up

  1. 1SmartAdd

    Once per PC, ahead of time: install Consumers SmartAdd

    In the Product Selector, click your name, then SmartAdd, and click Download. Extract the zip, go to edge://extensions (Chrome: chrome://extensions), turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the consumers-quote-helper folder. The SmartAdd page then says it is installed, and later tells you when there is a newer version. If a browser has two copies (an old hand-loaded one and the Group Policy one), only the newer copy shows its button (1.5.4), but remove the old one anyway.

  2. 2Product Selector

    Sign in to the Product Selector

    Sign in with your NetSuite email and webstore password on the same PC and browser that has SmartAdd.

  3. 3SmartAdd

    SmartAdd turns on for the vendor sites

    The Product Selector sends every special-order product line's website (the PRD page's website link in NetSuite). SmartAdd works only on those vendor domains and does nothing on other sites.

2Open the vendor site

  1. 4Product Selector

    Click SPECIAL ORDER, then a category

    In the menu click SPECIAL ORDER. Under Browse by category pick one, for example Plumbing: Faucets. The PRODUCT LINES cards for that category show.

  2. 5Product Selector

    Click a product line card: Shop vendor site

    Each card with a website says Shop vendor site. Clicking it opens the vendor's website in a new tab, for the customer to browse.

3Customer picks products

  1. 6Vendor website

    Customer opens a product on the vendor site

    The customer browses the vendor's site as usual and opens the product page they want.

  2. 7Vendor websiteDecision

    SmartAdd read the item from the page?

    The Add to CPS Quote List button shows at the bottom right on every page of a product line's vendor site, and reads the page again on each click. On a product page SmartAdd fills in the model, name and usually the price: from the page's product data, or from the model and price printed next to the product name. On Fleurco it reads the model and total once every configurator choice is made; a build of several items (a door with a base, drain or Mix & Match Kit) is split into its parts, each at its own price (1.5.5). Since 1.5.6 the same goes for Brizo combos and trims with their rough, Kohler and Delta bundles, Grohe required rough-ins, TOTO add-ons, Bain Ultra drain kits and options, Sofia accessories and John Boos add-ons. Since 1.5.7 it takes the list price, not a sale price, wherever the page shows both (Kohler's "List Price", Delta Outlet, Top Knobs specials, John Boos sales).

  3. 8SmartAdd

    Customer types the model and color in the panel

    A page with nothing to read (Avance's door styles, or Fleurco's shower door configurator before every choice is made) opens the panel with the page's name and a blank Model, with a note saying what to do. Type the model number and color shown on the page.

  4. 9Vendor website

    Check Type, model, finish, Coll/Desc, quantity; click Add to quote

    Choose the Option (color or finish) if the page does not say; picking another finish on the page greys the panel out with an Updating... spinner until it shows the new finish. Check the Type (the best fit is preselected), Model, Color / finish, Collection / Description (one field, as NetSuite's Coll/Desc), Price on this site and Quantity, then click Add to quote. Some sites show no price (Elkay, Franke, Strategic Brands); the rep enters the list price. On Richelieu the list price fills in from the Product Selector. A Fleurco kit shows one block per part and Add 4 items to quote puts each part on the Quote List as its own line. The price field is labeled List price. Report issue, at the left of the buttons, opens the same Report an Issue form as the Product Selector (Issue and Detail) and sends it to IT as a NetSuite case with a screenshot of the page, as the rep signed in to the Product Selector in this browser (it also shows in Admin, Issues & Bugs), and emails Jim, Gib, Phil and Gordon. Without a sign-in the panel says to open the Product Selector and sign in.

  5. 10SmartAdd

    Pick waits in SmartAdd for the sales rep

    The panel says Added to the CPS Quote List. N items waiting for your sales rep. The toolbar icon shows the count; its popup lists the waiting items and has Clear waiting items.

4Rep finishes each pick

  1. 11SmartAdd

    Pick cleared from SmartAdd

    As soon as it is on the Quote List, the pick leaves SmartAdd's waiting list and the counts go down.

  2. 12Product Selector

    Pick lands on the Quote List, Type suggested

    With the Product Selector open and signed in, each pick goes straight onto the Quote List as a special-order line: model, description, color, list price and quantity from the vendor page, the Type guessed from the product name, and a Picked on link to the vendor page. A pick whose site has several product lines (Foremost) waits on the Special Order page under Picked on vendor websites instead; Use fills in the form there.

  3. 13Product SelectorDecision

    Line shows an amber Needs ...?

    When the vendor page did not show everything NetSuite needs (a Type the name does not settle, a Color, the list price), the line says what is missing, and the Quote List will not push until it is done.

  4. 14Product Selector

    Click Needs ...: the form opens with the pick

    The Edit Special Order form opens with the product line, model, description, color and quantity filled in, and a link to the vendor page. The list price is blank when the page showed none.

  5. 15Product Selector

    Fill in what is missing, check the list price, click Save Changes

    Pick the Type if it is blank, type the Color or Model, and check the List Price: a vendor site can show a sale price (Delta Outlet items, for example). Price As starts on Retail.

5Send to the estimate

  1. 16Product Selector

    Send to NetSuite: search the estimate, click Add here

    Under Send these N items to NetSuite, type at least 3 digits of the estimate number (the ES is optional); names are not searched. Only open estimates show, by customer; click the estimate (Add here). Before you type, Sent to recently lists the last 5 records this browser sent to.

  2. 17Product Selector

    Check the black bar, click Add N items to ES###

    The bar says exactly what will happen, for example Add 3 items, $1,240.00, to ES###; Change picks another. If the estimate's customer is the other type (Retail or CRD) than a line's Price As, a note in the bar says it will be priced at the customer's type.

  3. 18NetSuite

    Special-order lines on the estimate, priced as in NetSuite

    Each line gets list price x the product line's multipliers for the estimate's customer (Retail or CRD), with its Type, Model, Coll/Desc and Color.

6Open Customer Search

  1. 19Product Selector

    Send to NetSuite: New estimate, then Find the customer in NetSuite

    Under Send these N items to NetSuite, click New estimate (under the search box, and under search results with no Sales Order in them), then Find the customer in NetSuite in the black bar. Finish any amber Needs ... first. Be signed in to NetSuite in the same browser; if the browser blocks the pop-up, allow pop-ups for the Product Selector and click again.

  2. 20Customer Search

    Customer Search opens with your Quote List

    The pop-up is NetSuite's customer search (the Sales Appointments one), titled Customer Search: new estimate from your Quote List. NetSuite reads the items from the Product Selector; only the rep who started it can use it, for 30 minutes. Non Customer Appt is hidden.

7Find or add the customer

  1. 21Customer Search

    Search by name, phone, email, customer number, zip or address

    Type in any of the search boxes; results show as you type.

  2. 22Customer SearchDecision

    Customer in the results?

    If they are, pick them. Never add someone who is already a customer.

  3. 23Customer Search

    Search by their full phone number or email

    + Add New Customer shows only when a search by the full 10-digit phone number or a valid email finds nobody, and nobody else has that phone or email. A search by name alone says: No customer found. To add a new customer, search by their full 10-digit phone number or their email address first.

  4. 24Customer Search

    Click the customer's row

    Click anywhere on the row (the name link opens the customer record instead).

  5. 25Customer Search

    + Add New Customer: fill in the form, Create Customer

    The form opens with the phone number or email from the search filled in and locked. Fill in the name, address and lead source as for an appointment.

  6. 26Customer Search

    Create an estimate for ...: your items listed

    The panel shows the customer and the Quote List items. Customers on Global Hold, Contact Hold or Discontinued, and inactive ones, are refused here with the reason; Choose a different customer goes back.

  7. 27Customer Search

    NetSuite checks for duplicates again and saves the Lead

    If the phone or email now belongs to someone, NetSuite refuses and names them: close the form, search for them and pick them. Otherwise the new customer is saved as a Lead and picked.

8Create the estimate

  1. 28Customer SearchDecision

    PRO (CRD) customer?

    PRO customers need the CRD User and Password, as on the estimate form.

  2. 29Customer Search

    Pick the CRD User, type the Password

    CRD User lists the customer's approved CRD contacts. The password must match that CRD user's password on file (INCORRECT Password clears it). COURTESY makes it a CRD - Courtesy Service estimate; CLIENT works as on the estimate form. The password is never logged.

  3. 30Customer Search

    Click Create Estimate

    Pressing it twice, or again after a lost connection, never makes a second estimate.

  4. 31NetSuite

    NetSuite creates the estimate, priced for this customer

    Form .Estimate with the usual defaults (your department and location, you as sales rep, close date in 30 days, the customer's category and email, ship method). Catalog items take NetSuite's price for the customer; special orders get the Retail or CRD price, as a push does. No opportunity is linked.

  5. 32Customer Search

    Pop-up says ES###### created

    Open ES###### opens the estimate in a new tab; Close window closes the pop-up.

9Back on the Quote List

  1. 33Product Selector

    Quote List: Created estimate ES...; items leave the list

    The Product Selector checks NetSuite for the estimate before anything leaves the list. If the pop-up was closed before it could tell the Quote List, closing it or Check NetSuite does the same check; Start over if NetSuite has none.

  2. 34Product Selector

    Discounts and deposit recalculated, as after a push

    The note under the message says when NetSuite has repriced the estimate's discounts and deposit.

10Find the order

  1. 35Product Selector

    Send to NetSuite: search the Sales Order number

    Under Send these N items to NetSuite, type at least 3 digits of the Sales Order number (SO is optional), or the SOR number. Only orders that can take a revision show, plus the one typed in full; an order with an open SOR shows dimmed, with the SOR on its own row under it (Use SOR###, or why it can't take items); an order with none open says Create new SOR, or why not.

  2. 36Product SelectorDecision

    Can this order take a new revision?

    The same rules as the order's New Revision button in NetSuite: the order is open (not Closed or Cancelled), no revision is open on it, no change order (COR) is waiting for verification, and no discount codes are entered but not Applied.

  3. 37Product Selector

    The order or its SOR says Can't take items now, and why

    The line under the order, or under its open SOR on its own row, says why, for example: SO### is Closed, so it can't take a revision; or SOR### is open on SO### (Submitted). A new revision can start once it is processed; or discount codes have not been Applied yet.

  4. 38Product Selector

    Click Create new SOR, then Create SOR with N items

    Shown only when the order has no open SOR. The black bar says Create a new SOR on SO### with N items. Finish any amber Needs ... first. If the order's customer is the other type (Retail or CRD) than a line's Price As, a note in the bar says it will be priced at the customer's type. Pressing it twice, or again after a lost connection, never starts a second revision.

  5. 39Product SelectorOptional

    A Draft SOR is open: its own row says Use SOR###

    When the open revision is a Draft that can take items, the order shows dimmed and the SOR is listed under it (SOR###, Revision of SO###, Draft) with Use SOR###. No new SOR is offered: an order has one open revision at a time. Clicking it is a normal push to that revision.

11NetSuite starts the revision

  1. 40NetSuite

    A Draft revision starts on the order, you as Requested By

    NetSuite starts it the same way the order's New Revision button does: it takes the order's snapshot and numbers the revision.

  2. 41NetSuite

    Your Quote List lines are added, priced as a push

    Catalog items at the customer's price level, special orders with the product line multipliers and their Type, Model, Coll/Desc and Color, all as Add lines waiting to be approved. If they can't all go on, the new empty revision is removed and nothing is left behind.

12Finish it in NetSuite

  1. 42Product Selector

    Created SOR### on SO###; the items leave your list

    The message has an Open SOR### in NetSuite link. If the answer was lost, Check again asks NetSuite before anything leaves the list; Start over if it has nothing.

  2. 43NetSuite

    NetSuite recalculates the revision's totals and discounts

    The note under the message says when it is done and shows the proposed total, as after a push to a revision.

  3. 44Product Selector

    Open SOR###: review, reprint, sign, Update Sales Order

    Finish the revision in NetSuite as usual. It must be printed and signed before Update Sales Order.

Not in the app yet

What this process needs that the app or SmartAdd does not do yet.

  1. Some pages leave fields for the customer. SmartAdd reads the item on 17 special-order vendor sites (checked page by page on 11/5/2026, about 230 product pages). Avance pages are door styles with no model numbers, so the customer types the model. Strategic Brands, Franke and Craft & Main show no price to the public, so the rep enters the list price. Richelieu's list price fills in from the Product Selector, which signs in to Richelieu on its server with Consumers' shared account (1.5.11); the rep's browser is never signed in to Richelieu. Configurators (Fleurco, Sofia, Bain Ultra) fill in once every choice is made.
  2. Picks stay in one browser. SmartAdd keeps picks on the PC where they were made. A customer browsing on their own phone or tablet cannot send picks, and the extension does not run on an iPad.
  3. Picks are not tied to a customer. Everything added on a PC lands in one waiting list. If two customers use the same PC, the rep has to tell their picks apart.
  4. The Type is only suggested when the name settles it. SmartAdd suggests the Type from the product name, the breadcrumbs and the vendor address (34 of 34 sweep cases right). When two Types fit equally the customer picks it; the panel requires it. Order-form Types (Bain Ultra tubs) go to NetSuite as Per Attached Order Form, so the rep attaches the vendor order form. Craft & Main shower bases and walls have no Type in NetSuite yet.
  5. New vendor sites need a SmartAdd release for the built-in list. SmartAdd goes to every PC by Group Policy and updates itself from the Product Selector. A product line made special-order in NetSuite works as soon as the Product Selector is open in that browser; the list built into SmartAdd (used before that) is refreshed with each release.
  6. A new estimate is not linked to an opportunity or appointment. The Customer Search pop-up creates the estimate on its own (Gib's choice, 11/11/2026). Link an opportunity on the estimate in NetSuite if the sale needs one.
  7. New SOR is open to every signed-in rep. In NetSuite only some roles see the New Revision button; the Product Selector does not know a rep's NetSuite role, so New SOR (like the push to a revision) works for anyone signed in to the Product Selector.
  8. A search takes several seconds. Each Send to NetSuite search asks NetSuite about every estimate and order it finds, and whether each order can take a revision, so results can take several seconds. A spinner shows in the search box meanwhile.

Good to know

  • After installing SmartAdd, open the Product Selector once while signed in so it gets the current vendor sites. The SmartAdd page (your name, then SmartAdd) shows whether this browser has it and if it is up to date.
  • SmartAdd's toolbar icon shows how many picks are waiting; its popup lists them and can clear them.
  • When a vendor's website changes, update the product line's website on its PRD page in NetSuite; SmartAdd follows the next time the Product Selector is open.
  • SmartAdd takes the list price where the vendor page shows one next to a sale price (since 1.5.7). Still check the List Price before Add to List.
  • New estimate needs NetSuite signed in, as the same rep, in the same browser as the Product Selector. Allow pop-ups for the Product Selector once.
  • Search new customers by their full phone number or email first: that is the only way + Add New Customer shows, so nobody is added twice.
  • Items for a Sales Order always go on a SOR. An order can have only one open revision: if it has one, the order shows dimmed with the SOR listed under it (Use SOR### for a Draft); Create new SOR shows only when none is open. New estimate is not offered under a Sales Order.
  • Send to NetSuite takes ES, SO or SOR numbers, not names. Closed, processed and expired records stay out of the list unless you type the whole number. Back to the classic Push to (at its bottom) brings back the four Push to buttons for this browser; Use the new Send to NetSuite returns.
