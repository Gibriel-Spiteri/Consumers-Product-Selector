From a product on a vendor's website to a priced special-order line on the estimate

Where each step happens: on the vendor's own website with the customer, in the Consumers Quote Helper browser extension (Edge or Chrome), in the Product Selector on the sales PC, and in NetSuite. Click any numbered box to read its exact buttons.

Step by step

1Set up

  1. 1Quote Helper

    Once per PC, ahead of time: install the Quote Helper

    In the Product Selector, click your name, then Quote Helper, and click Download. Extract the zip, go to edge://extensions (Chrome: chrome://extensions), turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the consumers-quote-helper folder. The Quote Helper page then says it is installed, and later tells you when there is a newer version.

  2. 2Product Selector

    Sign in to the Product Selector

    Sign in with your NetSuite email and webstore password on the same PC and browser that has the Quote Helper.

  3. 3Quote Helper

    Helper turns on for the vendor sites

    The Product Selector sends every special-order product line's website (the PRD page's website link in NetSuite). The helper works only on those vendor domains and does nothing on other sites.

2Open the vendor site

  1. 4Product Selector

    Click SPECIAL ORDER, then a category

    In the menu click SPECIAL ORDER. Under Browse by category pick one, for example Plumbing: Faucets. The PRODUCT LINES cards for that category show.

  2. 5Product Selector

    Click a product line card: Shop vendor site

    Each card with a website says Shop vendor site. Clicking it opens the vendor's website in a new tab, for the customer to browse.

3Customer picks products

  1. 6Vendor website

    Customer opens a product on the vendor site

    The customer browses the vendor's site as usual and opens the product page they want.

  2. 7Vendor websiteDecision

    Helper read the item from the page?

    The Add to Consumers Quote button shows at the bottom right on every page of a product line's vendor site, and reads the page again on each click. On a product page the helper fills in the model, name and usually the price: from the page's product data, or from the model and price printed next to the product name. On Fleurco it reads the model and total once every configurator choice is made.

  3. 8Quote Helper

    Customer types the model and color in the panel

    A page with nothing to read (Avance's door styles, or Fleurco's shower door configurator before every choice is made) opens the panel with the page's name and a blank Model, with a note saying what to do. Type the model number and color shown on the page.

  4. 9Vendor website

    Check Type, model, finish, collection, quantity; click Add to quote

    Choose the Option (color or finish) if there are several; picking another finish on the page refreshes the panel. Check the Type (the best fit is preselected), Model, Color / finish, Collection, Description, Price on this site and Quantity, then click Add to quote. Some sites show no price (Elkay, Franke, Richelieu, Strategic Brands); the rep enters the list price.

  5. 10Quote Helper

    Pick waits in the helper for the sales rep

    The panel says Added to your Consumers quote. N items waiting for your sales rep. The toolbar icon shows the count; its popup lists the waiting items and has Clear waiting items.

4Rep finishes each pick

  1. 11Product Selector

    Pick lands on the Quote List, Type suggested

    With the Product Selector open and signed in, each pick goes straight onto the Quote List as a special-order line: model, description, color, list price and quantity from the vendor page, the Type guessed from the product name, and a Picked on link to the vendor page. A pick whose site has several product lines (Foremost) waits on the Special Order page under Picked on vendor websites instead; Use fills in the form there.

  2. 12Quote Helper

    Pick cleared from the helper

    As soon as it is on the Quote List, the pick leaves the helper's waiting list and the counts go down.

  3. 13Product SelectorDecision

    Line shows an amber Needs ...?

    When the vendor page did not show everything NetSuite needs (a Type the name does not settle, a Color, the list price), the line says what is missing, and the Quote List will not push until it is done.

  4. 14Product Selector

    Click Needs ...: the form opens with the pick

    The Edit Special Order form opens with the product line, model, description, color and quantity filled in, and a link to the vendor page. The list price is blank when the page showed none.

  5. 15Product Selector

    Fill in what is missing, check the list price, click Save Changes

    Pick the Type if it is blank, type the Color or Model, and check the List Price: a vendor site can show a sale price (Delta Outlet items, for example). Price As starts on Retail.

5Push to the estimate

  1. 16Product Selector

    Quote List: type 3 digits of the estimate, select it

    Under Push to NetSuite Estimate type at least 3 digits of the estimate number (the ES is optional). The search runs by itself; click the open estimate.

  2. 17Product Selector

    Click Push Items to Estimate

    If the estimate's customer is the other type (Retail or CRD) than a line's Price As, a note says it will be priced at the estimate's type.

  3. 18NetSuite

    Special-order lines on the estimate, priced as in NetSuite

    Each line gets list price x the product line's multipliers for the estimate's customer (Retail or CRD), with its Type, Model, Coll/Desc and Color.

Not in the app yet

What this process needs that the app or the helper does not do yet.

  1. Avance pages have nothing to read. The helper reads the item on 15 of the 16 vendor sites. Avance's pages are door styles with no model numbers, so the customer types the model in the panel. Elkay, Franke, Richelieu and Strategic Brands show no price; the rep enters the list price.
  2. Picks stay in one browser. The helper keeps picks on the PC where they were made. A customer browsing on their own phone or tablet cannot send picks, and the extension does not run on an iPad.
  3. Picks are not tied to a customer. Everything added on a PC lands in one waiting list. If two customers use the same PC, the rep has to tell their picks apart.
  4. The Type is only suggested when the name settles it. The Quote List guesses the Type from the product name and the vendor's address (KITCHEN FAUCET, TOILET, KNOBS, SHOWER DOOR). When two Types fit equally, like Elkay's KITCHEN SINK or BATH SINK, or the right one is an order-form Type, the rep picks it.
  5. The helper is still loaded by hand on each PC. Staff download it from the Product Selector (their name, then Quote Helper) and load it with Load unpacked; updates are a new download and Reload. Publishing it unlisted on Edge Add-ons would let IT push it to every showroom PC and update it automatically.

Good to know

  • After installing the helper, open the Product Selector once while signed in so it gets the current vendor sites. The Quote Helper page (your name, then Quote Helper) shows whether this browser has it and if it is up to date.
  • The helper's toolbar icon shows how many picks are waiting; its popup lists them and can clear them.
  • When a vendor's website changes, update the product line's website on its PRD page in NetSuite; the helper follows the next time the Product Selector is open.
  • A vendor site can show a sale price. The list price is what counts, so always check it before Add to List.
